# Privacy Policy: Rìcháng (日常)

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Rìcháng is a game for learning Taiwan Mandarin, made by Nextale Audio. This policy applies to the Rìcháng apps for iOS,
iPadOS, Android and Windows, and to its web version.

## In short

Rìcháng does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and
no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. Nextale Audio receives nothing from the app.

## Your progress stays on your device

Your progress (the cards you unlocked, your review schedule, your settings and your current run) is saved only on your
device, in the app's own storage or, for the web version, in your browser's storage. It is never sent anywhere. If you
delete the app, or clear the browser's data for the web version, this progress is deleted too.

You can export your progress to a file and import it again later. The file is created only when you ask for it, and it
stays where it is saved (on iPhone and iPad, in the Files app, under On My iPhone or On My iPad › 日常).

## Microphone and speech recognition

Pronunciation exercises can use the microphone. The app asks for your permission first; you can refuse, or withdraw
the permission at any time in your device's settings, and the rest of the game works without it.

- **Recording yourself**: the recording is kept in the device's memory so that you can listen to it, and is discarded
  when you leave the exercise. It is never saved to a file and never sent anywhere.
- **Checking your pronunciation**: the app asks your device's built-in speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone and iPad;
  Google's or your device maker's on Android) to write down what you said. This service is part of your device's
  operating system and follows its own privacy policy; depending on your device, it may process your voice on the
  provider's servers. Rìcháng only receives the resulting text, compares it with the expected word, and keeps nothing.

## Voices

The voices of the dialogues are recordings included in the app. When a word or a sentence is read with a synthesized
voice, Rìcháng uses the speech synthesis built into your device's operating system.

## Links

The app contains a few links, for example to its source code on GitHub and to the authors of the photos it uses. They
open in your web browser only when you tap them; the websites you then visit have their own privacy policies.

## Purchases

If you get Rìcháng from the App Store or another store, the purchase is handled by that store under its own privacy
policy. Nextale Audio does not receive your payment details.

## Children

Rìcháng collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Its full
history is public in the project's repository.
